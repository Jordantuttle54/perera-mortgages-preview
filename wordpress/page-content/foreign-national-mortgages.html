Foreign national mortgage questions

Can I apply for a UK mortgage using income earned overseas?

It depends on the lender’s criteria, the type and currency of your income, and the evidence available. We can discuss your circumstances and explain what needs checking before options are presented.

Will my visa or residency circumstances affect an application?

They may. Lenders can apply different requirements based on an applicant’s circumstances. We’ll establish the relevant details and explain what needs to be checked for your case.

Does a limited UK credit history mean I cannot get a mortgage?

A limited UK credit history does not, by itself, determine what may be possible. Lenders assess applications differently, so it is considered alongside your residency, income, deposit and plans.

Can I use savings held outside the UK for my deposit?

This depends on the lender’s requirements and the details of the funds. We’ll discuss where your deposit is held, how it was built up and what evidence may be needed.
