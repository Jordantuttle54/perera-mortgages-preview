Buying a UK home or investment property can raise extra questions when you are a foreign national, earn income overseas or have a visa or residency status that differs from a UK citizen. The right approach depends on your full circumstances and the criteria of the lenders available to consider them.

Mortgage advice for international circumstances

Perera Mortgages provides personal mortgage advice for foreign nationals looking to buy property in the UK. We start by understanding your plans, income, residency position and deposit, then explain what may be possible and what information a lender could need.

Foreign national mortgage applications are not all assessed in the same way. A lender may consider the property and how it will be used, your visa or residency circumstances, the currency and source of your income, your UK credit history, and the deposit or assets available. The details that matter will depend on the application.

The details we’ll look at with you

  • Income earned overseas or in another currency. We’ll discuss how you’re paid and what evidence may be available.
  • Visa or residency circumstances. We’ll establish your current position and the information a lender may ask for.
  • A limited UK credit history. We’ll consider this alongside the wider application rather than treating it as the whole picture.
  • International assets and savings. We’ll discuss your deposit and the source of funds, including any international accounts or assets relevant to the purchase.

Questions about foreign national mortgages

Can I apply for a UK mortgage using overseas income?

It depends on the lender’s criteria, the type and currency of your income, and the evidence available. We can discuss your situation and explain what needs checking before any options are presented.

Will my visa or residency status affect a mortgage application?

It may. Lenders can apply different requirements based on an applicant’s circumstances. We’ll establish the relevant details and explain what needs to be checked for your case.

Does a limited UK credit history mean I cannot get a mortgage?

A limited UK credit history does not, on its own, tell us what may be possible. Lenders assess applications differently, so we’ll consider it alongside your residency, income, deposit and plans.

Can I use savings held outside the UK for my deposit?

This depends on the lender’s requirements and the details of the funds. We’ll discuss where your deposit is held, how it was built up and what evidence may be needed.
